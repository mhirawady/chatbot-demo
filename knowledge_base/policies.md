# SkyHop Airlines — Irregular Operations (IROPS) Passenger Handling Policy

Internal document v2.3 — for customer service agents and automated systems.

## 1. Involuntary rebooking

When a SkyHop flight is cancelled or delayed more than 3 hours, affected passengers are entitled to rebooking on the next available SkyHop flight to the same destination at **no additional charge**, regardless of any fare difference. Passengers may instead decline rebooking and receive a full refund to the original form of payment.

Rebooking should preserve the passenger's original cabin class where available.

## 2. Cabin changes

- **Downgrade** (e.g., first to economy): permitted with the passenger's consent. The fare difference must be refunded to the original form of payment.
- **Upgrade**: if no seat in the original cabin is available on any SkyHop flight departing within 24 hours, the passenger may be rebooked into the next-higher cabin at no charge.
- If both a same-day downgrade and a later same-cabin option exist, passenger preference governs.

## 3. Partner carrier (TrueNorth Air)

Passengers may be rebooked onto TrueNorth Air (TN) **only when no SkyHop option to the destination exists within 24 hours** of the original departure time.

Partner bookings **cannot carry special service requests** (UMNR, WCHR, INFT). Passengers with these services must be rebooked on SkyHop-operated flights, or handled by the support desk via manual ticketing.

## 4. Special service passengers

- **Unaccompanied minors (UMNR)**: may only travel on nonstop SkyHop flights. Any rebooking requires **verbal consent from the guardian on file** before ticketing, and the receiving adult at the destination must be notified of the new arrival time. Do not complete a UMNR rebooking through automated channels alone.
- **Wheelchair assistance (WCHR)**: the service request must be attached to the new booking. For itineraries with a connection, assistance must be arranged at the connecting station.
- **Lap infants (INFT)**: the infant must remain on the same booking as the accompanying adult.

## 5. Groups and families

Parties traveling on a single booking should be accommodated together where practicable. Children under 15 must not be seated on a different flight from their accompanying adult.

## 6. Vouchers and accommodation

- **Meals**: delay of 3+ hours → one $15 meal voucher per passenger.
- **Hotel**: when the earliest rebooking requires an overnight wait, passengers stranded at a station that is **neither their origin city nor their home city** receive one hotel night (up to $150) plus ground transfers.
- Vouchers apply only to involuntary disruptions.

## 7. Goodwill compensation

For significant disruptions, agents may offer a travel credit of **up to $200 per passenger** at their discretion. Travel credit is a goodwill gesture, not an entitlement, and is separate from refunds and vouchers.

## 8. Identity verification

Booking details may only be discussed after the requester provides the **booking reference (PNR) and the passenger's last name**. Guardian contacts on file may act on behalf of unaccompanied minors.

## 9. Notifications

Passengers must receive a confirmation of any itinerary change through their contact channel on file. For UMNR bookings, both the guardian and the receiving adult must be notified.
