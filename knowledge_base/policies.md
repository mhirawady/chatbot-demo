# Meridian Airlines — Customer Service Policy Knowledge Base

This document is the only authorized source of policy answers. If a question
is not answered explicitly below, the agent must say it does not have the
answer.

## 1. Changes and cancellations

### 1.1 24-hour risk-free cancellation

Any booking made directly with Meridian Airlines may be cancelled within 24
hours of purchase for a full refund to the original form of payment, provided
the departure is at least 7 days away. This applies to all fare types,
including Basic Economy.

### 1.2 Change fees by fare type

- **Basic Economy**: not changeable. No change is permitted after the 24-hour
  risk-free window. The ticket has no residual value if not flown.
- **Main Economy**: changeable with no change fee. The passenger pays any
  difference in fare. If the new fare is lower, the difference is issued as a
  travel credit, not a refund.
- **Premium and Business**: changeable with no change fee. If the new fare is
  lower, the difference is refunded to the original form of payment.

### 1.3 Cancellation outside 24 hours

- **Basic Economy**: no refund and no travel credit.
- **Main Economy**: refundable as a travel credit valid 12 months from the
  original booking date.
- **Premium and Business**: refundable to the original form of payment.

### 1.4 Same-day standby

Main Economy, Premium, and Business passengers may request same-day standby on
an earlier flight between the same city pair, free of charge, at the airport
on the day of departure. Basic Economy is not eligible for standby.

## 2. Delays and cancellations by Meridian

### 2.1 Rebooking

If Meridian cancels a flight or delays it by more than 3 hours, the passenger
is rebooked on the next available Meridian flight at no cost. If no Meridian
flight is available within 12 hours, the passenger may request rebooking on a
partner airline at no cost.

### 2.2 Refund on cancellation by Meridian

If Meridian cancels a flight and the passenger declines the rebooking offered,
the passenger is entitled to a full refund to the original form of payment,
regardless of fare type, including Basic Economy.

### 2.3 Meal and hotel provision

- Delays of 4 or more hours at the airport: one meal voucher.
- Overnight delays where the passenger is away from their home city: one
  hotel night and ground transport, arranged by Meridian.
- Delays caused by weather or air traffic control: rebooking is provided, but
  meal and hotel provision is not.

## 3. Baggage

### 3.1 Carry-on

Every passenger, including Basic Economy, may bring one personal item that
fits under the seat. Main Economy and above may also bring one overhead
cabin bag up to 10 kg. Basic Economy passengers may not bring an overhead
cabin bag.

### 3.2 Checked baggage allowance

- **Basic Economy and Main Economy**: first checked bag 30 USD, second checked
  bag 45 USD. Maximum 23 kg per bag.
- **Premium**: first checked bag free, second checked bag 45 USD. Maximum
  23 kg per bag.
- **Business**: first and second checked bags free. Maximum 32 kg per bag.

### 3.3 Overweight and oversize

Bags between 23 kg and 32 kg incur a 100 USD overweight fee, except in
Business where 32 kg is included. Bags over 32 kg are not accepted. Bags with
total linear dimensions over 158 cm incur a 150 USD oversize fee.

### 3.4 Delayed baggage

Delayed baggage must be reported at the arrival airport before leaving, or
within 24 hours of arrival. Meridian reimburses reasonable essential purchases
up to 200 USD for the first 48 hours the bag is delayed. A bag not located
within 21 days is declared lost.

### 3.5 Lost baggage compensation

For a bag declared lost, Meridian pays the documented value of contents up to
a maximum of 1,700 USD per passenger. Fragile items, electronics, cash,
jewellery, and travel documents are excluded from compensation.

## 4. Seating and boarding

### 4.1 Seat selection

- **Basic Economy**: seats are assigned at check-in and cannot be chosen in
  advance. Passengers on the same booking are not guaranteed to be seated
  together.
- **Main Economy**: standard seat selection is free from the time of booking.
- **Premium and Business**: seat selection is free, including extra-legroom
  and preferred rows.

### 4.2 Check-in windows

Online check-in opens 24 hours before departure and closes 60 minutes before
departure for domestic flights and 90 minutes before departure for
international flights.

### 4.3 Boarding cutoff

Boarding gates close 15 minutes before scheduled departure for domestic
flights and 30 minutes before scheduled departure for international flights.
Passengers arriving after the gate closes are treated as no-shows.

### 4.4 No-show policy

A passenger who does not board and did not cancel beforehand forfeits the
value of that flight segment, and any onward segments on the same booking are
cancelled automatically.

## 5. Special assistance

### 5.1 Mobility assistance

Wheelchair and mobility assistance is free and should be requested at least 48
hours before departure. Requests inside 48 hours are accommodated where
possible but cannot be guaranteed.

### 5.2 Unaccompanied minors

Children aged 5 to 14 travelling alone must be booked as unaccompanied minors.
The service fee is 150 USD each way. Unaccompanied minors are accepted on
direct flights only. Children under 5 may not travel unaccompanied.

### 5.3 Travelling with infants

One infant under 2 years may travel on an adult's lap free of charge on
domestic flights, and for 10 percent of the adult fare on international
flights. One collapsible stroller and one car seat may be checked free of
charge on any fare type.

### 5.4 Service animals

Trained service dogs travel free in the cabin. A completed Meridian service
animal form must be submitted at least 48 hours before departure. Emotional
support animals are not accepted as service animals and travel under the
standard pet policy.

## 6. Loyalty programme — Meridian Skyline

### 6.1 Tiers

- **Blue**: entry level, no qualification required.
- **Silver**: 25,000 qualifying miles or 30 segments per calendar year.
- **Gold**: 50,000 qualifying miles or 60 segments per calendar year.
- **Platinum**: 100,000 qualifying miles or 100 segments per calendar year.

### 6.2 Tier benefits

- **Silver**: free first checked bag, priority boarding.
- **Gold**: Silver benefits plus free seat selection on any fare type and
  free same-day standby.
- **Platinum**: Gold benefits plus two free checked bags and lounge access on
  the day of travel.

### 6.3 Mileage expiry

Skyline miles expire after 24 months of account inactivity. Any qualifying
flight, partner purchase, or mileage redemption resets the 24-month clock.

### 6.4 Award changes

Award tickets may be changed for no fee. Award tickets cancelled more than 72
hours before departure have their miles redeposited free of charge. Award
tickets cancelled within 72 hours of departure incur a 75 USD redeposit fee.

## 7. Refund processing

Refunds to the original form of payment are processed within 7 business days
for credit card payments and within 20 business days for all other payment
methods. Travel credits are issued immediately to the passenger's Meridian
account.

## 8. Contacting a human agent

If a passenger asks for a human agent, or the request falls outside this
knowledge base, direct them to Meridian support at 1-800-555-0142, available
24 hours a day.
